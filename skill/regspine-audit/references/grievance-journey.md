# Complaint / Grievance Journey — rulebook

Sources: RB-IOS 2021 (Integrated Ombudsman Scheme); Fair Practices Code (grievance
mechanism + nodal officer); Digital Lending Directions (grievance for DLA journeys); DPDP
(data-grievance route). A complaint journey is itself a regulated journey — and the one
regulators actually walk through when testing an app.

## Entry & visibility

- Grievance entry point reachable from every major screen (persistent help/footer), not
  buried three menus deep. FPC requires the mechanism to be publicised.
- Display: **Grievance Redressal Officer / Nodal Officer name, contact (email + phone)** for
  Butter (as LSP) AND for the lending RE / insurer as applicable. On digital lending
  journeys this is a Directions requirement, not a nicety.
- Plain-language category picker (loan, insurance, payments, data/privacy, other) — the
  data/privacy category routes to the DPDP grievance path.

## Filing screen

- Free-text + attachments; don't force phone calls for filing.
- Confirm what happens next before submission: expected TAT, how updates arrive.
- On submit: **unique complaint/reference number**, on-screen + via SMS/email/WhatsApp.
- No dark patterns in reverse: cancellation/complaint paths must not be harder than
  purchase paths (obstruction is a CCPA-named dark pattern).

## Tracking & TAT

- Status tracker with honest states (received → in review → action → resolved) and dates.
- **30-day rule**: if the RE doesn't resolve within 30 days (or the reply is unsatisfactory),
  the customer can escalate to the **RBI Ombudsman (CMS portal — cms.rbi.org.in)**. The
  journey must SAY this at the right moment: on filing confirmation and again at/after the
  30-day mark. Hiding the escalation right = 🔴.
- For insurance complaints: parallel path to insurer's GRO, then **Bima Bharosa / Insurance
  Ombudsman** — surface the correct regulator's route per product.

## Resolution & closure

- Resolution message states what was done and the user's options if unsatisfied (escalation
  routes, with links).
- Closure requires communication — silent auto-closure = 🔴 conduct issue.
- Post-resolution feedback ask is fine; making feedback a condition of seeing the
  resolution is not.

## CX levers that are compliance-positive

Proactive status pushes beat inbound "where is my complaint" calls; showing the 30-day
escalation clock as a visible countdown builds trust AND satisfies the disclosure duty;
pre-filled complaint context (loan ID, screen where issue occurred) cuts resolution time.
